Child Safety Standards
Last updated: June 8, 2026

VidMood is committed to maintaining a safe environment for all users, especially minors. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content that exploits or endangers children.

1. Minimum Age Requirement

VidMood is intended for users aged 13 years and older. Users under 13 are not permitted to create accounts. If we become aware that a user is under 13, we will immediately suspend the account and delete associated data.

2. Prohibited Content

Zero Tolerance

The following content is strictly prohibited on VidMood:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) of any kind
  • Content that sexualizes minors in any way
  • Grooming, exploitation, or solicitation of minors
  • Content that encourages or facilitates harm to children
  • Any content that violates child safety laws

3. Detection and Moderation

  • All uploaded content is subject to automated scanning and human moderation
  • We use industry-standard hash-matching technology to detect known CSAM
  • Users can report any content or account that violates child safety standards
  • Reports are reviewed within 24 hours by our moderation team
  • Violating accounts are immediately suspended pending investigation

4. Reporting to Authorities

Legal Compliance

VidMood complies with all applicable child safety laws. Any confirmed CSAM discovered on our platform is immediately reported to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline, as well as to relevant local and national law enforcement authorities. We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

5. How to Report

Users can report child safety concerns directly within the app:

  • Tap the three dots (···) on any video, profile or comment
  • Select "Report"
  • Choose "Child Safety" as the reason

You can also report directly by email: madatsi50@gmail.com

6. Parental Controls

We encourage parents to:

  • Monitor their children's use of social media applications
  • Use Android's built-in parental controls (Google Family Link)
  • Report any concerns to us immediately
